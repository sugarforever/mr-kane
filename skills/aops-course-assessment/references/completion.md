# Lesson completion

A lesson is an independently planned and assessed learning unit. The current AoPS adapter uses selected chapter scope and chapter courseIds; other source groupings require adapter support.

For each objective use its declared `masteryCriteria`, established during planning or an explicit unfinished-plan revision. Require actual independently correct student evidence covering the full criterion, with explanation or transfer when the target demands it. One integrated task may support several objectives; judge coverage and mathematical validity, not task counts or an all-correct score alone. The server verifies at least one independently correct attempt per declared objective and source provenance; whether the criterion is actually satisfied remains an agent judgement.

Legacy objectives without masteryCriteria retain the default: independently correct routine application plus independently correct explanation or transfer on a different task. Preserve that default until the working plan is explicitly revised, retaining IDs, historical attempts and completed targets. Do not lower requirements solely to accept an unsuccessful attempt.

Use exact student-answer excerpts and original assistance levels. Hinted work, self-report, reading and demonstrations do not establish independent mastery. Reuse equivalent prior independent evidence without re-teaching. Delayed review checks retention after advancement rather than requiring immediate repetitive drills.

Completion requires every objective's criteria, status `completed`, phase `report`, and a report interpreting the evidence and next step. Readiness to learn a subsequent topic is distinct from formal completion. Missing evidence leaves the lesson incomplete; a placement-based scope omission is a waiver, while demonstrated mastery can complete a lesson through a brief summary.
