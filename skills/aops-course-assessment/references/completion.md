# Lesson completion

One lesson is one AoPS chapter with stated objectives. For every objective, require independently correct routine application plus independently correct explanation or transfer on a different task. Judge whether both tasks cover the objective and resolve observed misconceptions; satisfying the evidence categories alone is insufficient.

Use exact student-answer excerpts from user messages and retain assistance levels. Hinted work, self-report, reading and tutor demonstrations do not establish independent mastery.

A completed lesson has status `completed`, phase `report`, and a report summarizing the objective evidence and next step. Missing evidence leaves the lesson incomplete.
