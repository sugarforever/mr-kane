# Lesson completion

For each objective apply its `masteryCriteria`. Require independently correct student work covering the full requirement, including reasoning or transfer where specified. One integrated task may cover several objectives. Judge actual coverage and validity; an all-correct score alone is insufficient.

For legacy objectives without `masteryCriteria`, require independent routine application plus independent explanation or transfer on a different task. Change criteria only through an explicit unfinished-plan revision justified by the learning goal and scope, not by a desire to accept an unsuccessful attempt. Preserve completed targets.

Reuse prior evidence for equivalent objectives with exact student excerpts and original assistance levels. Self-report, reading, demonstrations and prompted agreement establish exposure. Hinted work remains assisted; re-solving a demonstrated task alone does not establish transfer.

Complete only when every objective meets its criterion, phase is `report`, status is `completed`, and the report explains the evidence and next step. Retain incomplete targets even when prerequisites allow another activity. Recommend delayed review for retention after advancement.

The server checks evidence presence and excerpt provenance. For declared criteria it requires at least one independently correct attempt per objective; mathematical correctness and full criterion coverage remain agent judgements.
