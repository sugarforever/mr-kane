# IrMO 2018 · 12 May 2018

## Q1 | Modular number game | number-theory

Mary picks an integer greater than $2017$, multiplies it by $2017$, and adds $2$. Pat adds $2019$ to the result. They alternate, Mary always multiplying the current number by $2017$ and adding $2$, Pat always adding $2019$. Pat wins if any number obtained is divisible by $2018$. Determine with proof the smallest initial integer Mary can choose to prevent Pat from winning.

## Q2 | Incentre perpendiculars | geometry

Triangle $ABC$ is right-angled at $A$, with incentre $I$. Let $H$ be the foot of the perpendicular from $I$ to $AB$. The perpendicular from $H$ to $BC$ meets $BC$ at $E$ and the bisector of $\angle ABC$ at $D$. The perpendicular from $A$ to $BC$ meets it at $F$. Prove $\angle EFD=45^\circ$.

## Q3 | Quadratic function composition | algebra

Find all functions $f(x)=ax^2+bx+c$, $a\ne0$, such that $f(f(1))=f(f(0))=f(f(-1))$.

## Q4 | Nested rectangles | combinatorics

A rectangle of sides $a,b$ fits inside one of sides $c,d$ if either $a\le c,b\le d$ or $a\le d,b\le c$. A set $S$ contains $2019$ rectangles with integer side lengths between $1$ and $2018$ inclusive. Show there are three rectangles $A,B,C$ in $S$ with $A$ fitting inside $B$ and $B$ inside $C$.

## Q5 | Tangents from circle points | geometry

$A,B,P$ lie on circle $\Omega_1$ with $\angle APB$ obtuse. $Q$ is the foot of the perpendicular from $P$ to $AB$. Circle $\Omega_2$ has centre $P$ and radius $PQ$. The tangents from $A,B$ to $\Omega_2$ intersect $\Omega_1$ at $F,H$, respectively. Prove $FH$ is tangent to $\Omega_2$.

## Q6 | Real functional equation | algebra

Find all real-valued functions $f$ such that
$$f(2x+f(y))+f(f(y))=4x+8y$$
for all real $x,y$.

## Q7 | Triangle side inequality | inequalities, geometry

If $a,b,c$ are the side lengths of a triangle, prove
$$2(a^3+b^3+c^3)<(a+b+c)(a^2+b^2+c^2)\le3(a^3+b^3+c^3).$$

## Q8 | Equilateral triangle extensions | geometry

$M$ is the midpoint of $BC$ in equilateral triangle $ABC$. $D$ lies on $CA$ extended with $A$ between $D,C$. $E$ lies on $AB$ extended with $B$ between $A,E$ and $|MD|=|ME|$. Let $F=MD\cap AB$. Prove $\angle BFM=\angle BME$.

## Q9 | Cubes in a recurrence | sequences, number-theory

A positive integer sequence satisfies $a_{n+1}=a_n^2+2018$ for $n\ge1$. Prove at most one term is a cube of an integer.

## Q10 | Greed stone game | combinatorics

Greed starts with one or more piles of stones. Player 1 starts; the two players alternate. Each turn one may remove one stone from one pile (simple move), or one stone from each remaining pile (greedy move). Whoever takes the last stone wins. Find a strategy guaranteeing victory to one player for each initial configuration:

(a) $2018$ piles, each containing either $20$ or $18$ stones.

(b) Four piles containing $17,18,19,20$ stones, respectively.
