# IrMO 2021 · 8 May 2021

## Q1 | Factorial as nine factors | number-theory

Let $N=15!=15\cdot14\cdots2\cdot1$. Prove $N$ is a product of nine distinct integers all between $16$ and $30$ inclusive.

## Q2 | Isosceles right triangle chords | geometry

Isosceles triangle $ABC$ is inscribed in a circle with $\angle ACB=90^\circ$. Chord $EF$ has neither endpoint equal to $C$. Lines $CE,CF$ meet $AB$ at $D,G$, respectively. Prove $|CE|\,|DG|=|EF|\,|CG|$.

## Q3 | Moving two leading digits | number-theory

For each integer $n\ge100$, let $T(n)$ be obtained by moving its two leading digits to the end. For example $T(12345)=34512$ and $T(100)=10$. Find all integers $n\ge100$ such that $n+T(n)=10n$.

## Q4 | Domino tilings modulo nineteen | combinatorics, number-theory

A $3\times2021$ chessboard has one corner square removed. There are $3031$ identical dominoes, each covering two adjacent squares. If $m$ counts coverings without gaps or overlaps, what is the remainder of $m$ divided by $19$?

## Q5 | Iterated fractional function | algebra

A function $g:[0,\infty)\to[0,\infty)$ satisfies
$$g(g(x))=\frac{3x}{x+3}\quad(x\ge0),\qquad g(x)=\frac{x+1}{2}\quad(2\le x\le3).$$
Find (a) $g(2021)$; (b) $g(1/2021)$.

## Q6 | Areas in arithmetic progression | sequences, geometry

A sequence has a positive first term, and each following term is the area of an equilateral triangle whose perimeter is the preceding term. If the first three terms form an arithmetic progression, determine all possible first terms.

## Q7 | Squared row and column totals | combinatorics, algebra

An $n\times n$ grid is coloured red or blue, with $k$ blue cells. Let $S_B$ be the sum of the squares of the blue-cell counts in all rows plus that in all columns. Define $S_R$ similarly for red cells. If $S_B-S_R=50$, determine with proof all possible $k$.

## Q8 | Common tangent concurrency | geometry

$C$ lies between $A,B$ on segment $AB$. Circles have diameters $AC,CB$. A common tangent touches them at $P\ne C,Q\ne C$, respectively. Prove $AP,BQ$ and the common tangent at $C$ meet at one point on the circle with diameter $AB$.

## Q9 | Absolute value product inequality | inequalities

Real $a,A,b,B$ satisfy $a,b>0$, $|A-3a|\le1-a$, $|B-3b|\le1-b$. Prove
$$\left|\frac{AB}{3}-3ab\right|-3ab\le1-ab.$$

## Q10 | Nearby points with fixed centroid | combinatorics, geometry

Points $P_1,\ldots,P_{2021}$ lie in $\{(x,y):x\ge0,y\ge0\}$ with centroid $(1,1)$. Prove two distinct points have distance at most $\sqrt2/20$.
