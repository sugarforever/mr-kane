# IrMO 2022 · 7 May 2022

## Q1 | Factorial plus three | number-theory

For positive $n$, $n!=1\cdot2\cdots n$. Determine with proof all positive integers $n$ for which $n!+3$ is a power of $3$.

## Q2 | Square and tangent ratio | geometry

$ABCD$ is a square and $\Gamma$ is the circle with diameter $CD$. A tangent from $B$ touches $\Gamma$ at $E$ and meets segment $AD$ at $K$. Prove $|AD|=4|KD|$.

## Q3 | Permutation turning points | combinatorics, number-theory

For $n\ge3$, let $(p_1,\ldots,p_n)$ be a permutation of $\{1,\ldots,n\}$. A turning point is $p_t$ where $2\le t\le n-1$ and $(p_t-p_{t-1})(p_t-p_{t+1})>0$. For example $(2,4,6,7,5,1,3,8)$ has turning points $p_4=7,p_6=1$. Let $q(n)$ count permutations with exactly one turning point. Find all $n\ge3$ for which $q(n)$ is a perfect square.

## Q4 | Increasing iterated function | algebra

For strictly positive integers $\mathbb N$, a function $f:\mathbb N\to\mathbb N$ satisfies $f(n)<f(n+1)$ and $f(f(f(n)))=4n$ for every $n\in\mathbb N$. Find $f(2022)$.

## Q5 | Circumcentre and parallelogram | geometry

Triangle $ABC$ has circumcentre $O$. The perpendicular from $O$ to $BC$ meets $BC$ at $M$ and $AC$ at $P$. The perpendicular from $O$ to $AC$ meets $AC$ at $N$ and $BC$ at $Q$. Let $D=PQ\cap MN$. Construct parallelogram $PCQJ$ with $PJ\parallel CQ$, $QJ\parallel CP$. Prove (a) $A,B,O,P,Q,J$ are concyclic; (b) $OD\perp CJ$.

## Q6 | Cubic identity | algebra

For real $a,b,c$ with $a+b+c=1$, prove
$$a^3+b^3+c^3+3(1-a)(1-b)(1-c)=1.$$

## Q7 | Cyclic quadrilateral perpendicularity | geometry

$A,B,C,D$ lie on the circle with diameter $AB$. Their diagonals meet at $E$, and lines $AD,BC$ meet at $F$. Line $FE$ meets $AB$ at $K$, and $DK$ meets the circle again at $L$. Prove $CL\perp AB$.

## Q8 | Distinct digit multiplication | number-theory, combinatorics

In $\overline{AB}\times\overline{CD}=\overline{EFGH}$ each letter is a distinct digit and $A,C,E$ are nonzero. An example is $46\times85=3910$. Find the smallest possible four-digit product $\overline{EFGH}$.

## Q9 | Recurrence adjacent products | sequences, number-theory

For positive integer $k$, define $x_0=0$, $x_1=1$, $x_{n+1}=kx_n+x_{n-1}$ for $n\ge1$. Prove $x_{n-1}x_{n+1}$ is never a perfect square for $n\ge2$, first (a) for $k=1$, then (b) for every $k\ge1$.

## Q10 | Maximum minimum score gap | combinatorics

Let $n\ge5$ be odd and $1\le r\le(n-1)/2$ an integer. In a tournament each subset of $2r+1$ players among $n$ plays exactly one contest. Each contest assigns the scores $-r,-(r-1),\ldots,-1,0,1,\ldots,r$ in some order. Player $i$ has total $S_i$. Determine with proof the maximum possible value of $N=\min_{i<j}|S_i-S_j|$.
