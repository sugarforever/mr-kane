# IrMO 2014 · 10 May 2014

## Q1 | Chessboard selections | combinatorics

In how many ways can $56$ squares be selected on an $8\times8$ chessboard so that (a) all black squares are selected; (b) exactly seven squares are selected in each row and each column?

## Q2 | Polynomial divisibility | number-theory, algebra

Prove for every integer $N>1$ that $(N^2)^{2014}-(N^{11})^{106}$ is divisible by $N^6+N^3+1$.

## Q3 | Trisected triangle angle | geometry

In triangle $ABC$, $D$ is the foot of the altitude from $A$ to $BC$ and $M$ is the midpoint of $BC$. The angles $\angle BAD,\angle DAM,\angle MAC$ are equal. Find the angles of $ABC$.

## Q4 | Cyclic equations | algebra

Three distinct nonzero real numbers $a,b,c$ satisfy
$$a+\frac2b=b+\frac2c=c+\frac2a=p.$$
Prove $abc+2p=0$.

## Q5 | Normalised squares inequality | inequalities

Suppose $a_1,\ldots,a_n>0$, $n>1$ and $\sum_{i=1}^n a_i=1$. Put $b_i=a_i^2/\sum_{j=1}^n a_j^2$. Prove
$$\sum_{i=1}^n\frac{a_i}{1-a_i}\le\sum_{i=1}^n\frac{b_i}{1-b_i}.$$
When does equality occur?

## Q6 | Consecutive integers with six divisors | number-theory

Each of $N,N+1,N+2,N+3$ has exactly six positive divisors. Exactly $20$ distinct positive numbers divide at least one of them, and one is $27$. Find all possible $N$. Both $1$ and $m$ count as divisors of $m$.

## Q7 | Square chord ratios | geometry

Square $ABCD$ is inscribed in a circle with centre $O$. $E$ is the midpoint of $AD$. Line $CE$ meets the circle again at $F$. Lines $FB,AD$ meet at $H$. Prove $|HD|=2|AH|$.

## Q8 | Integer valued polynomials | algebra, number-theory

(a) Let $P(x)=a_0+a_1x+a_2x^2$ with real coefficients. If $P(-1),P(0),P(1)$ are integers, prove $P(n)$ is an integer for every integer $n$.

(b) Let $Q(x)=a_0+a_1x+a_2x^2+a_3x^3$ with real coefficients. If $Q(i),Q(i+1),Q(i+2),Q(i+3)$ are integers for some integer $i$, prove $Q(n)$ is an integer for every integer $n$.

## Q9 | Negative polynomial coefficients | algebra, inequalities

Let $n>0$ and $a_1,\ldots,a_n>0$. Put $g(x)=(x+a_1)\cdots(x+a_n)$. For real $a_0$ let
$$f(x)=(x-a_0)g(x)=x^{n+1}+b_1x^n+b_2x^{n-1}+\cdots+b_nx+b_{n+1}.$$
Prove all $b_1,\ldots,b_{n+1}$ are negative if and only if $a_0>a_1+\cdots+a_n$.

## Q10 | Consecutive birth totals | combinatorics

Over $k$ consecutive days, $2014$ babies were born in a city, with at least one born each day. Show:

(a) If $1014<k\le2014$, some period of consecutive days has exactly $100$ births.

(b) If $k=1014$, such a period might not exist.
