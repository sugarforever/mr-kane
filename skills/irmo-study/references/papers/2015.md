# IrMO 2015 · 25 April 2015

## Q1 | Median length range | geometry

In triangle $ABC$ the altitude from $A$ to $BC$ has length $1$. $D$ is the midpoint of $AC$. What are the possible lengths of $BD$?

## Q2 | Three colour triangulation | combinatorics, geometry

A regular polygon with $n\ge3$ sides has each vertex coloured red, green or blue, with no adjacent vertices of the same colour and at least one vertex of each colour. Prove that diagonals can be drawn, intersecting only at vertices, dividing the polygon into triangles each having vertices of three different colours.

## Q3 | Two cubes around 837 | number-theory

Find all positive integers $n$ for which both $837+n$ and $837-n$ are cubes of positive integers.

## Q4 | Touching circles and chords | geometry

Circles $C_1,C_2$ with centres $D,E$ touch at $B$. The circle with diameter $DE$ intersects $C_1$ at $H$ and $C_2$ at $K$, on the same side of $DE$. Line $HK$ extended meets $C_1$ at $L$ and $C_2$ at $M$. Prove (a) $|LH|=|KM|$; (b) the perpendicular to $DE$ through $B$ bisects $HK$.

## Q5 | Bounded doubly infinite recurrence | sequences

A doubly infinite real sequence $\ldots,a_{-1},a_0,a_1,\ldots$ satisfies
$$a_{n+3}=\frac{a_n+a_{n+1}+a_{n+2}}3$$
for every integer $n$. Show that if it is bounded (there is an $R$ with $|a_n|\le R$ for all $n$), it is constant.

## Q6 | Quadratic inequality | inequalities

For $x,y\ge0$ with $x+y\le1$, prove $8xy\le5x(1-x)+5y(1-y)$ and determine the equality cases.

## Q7 | Sum free subsets | combinatorics

Let $n>1$ and $\Omega=\{1,2,\ldots,2n\}$. A nonempty subset $S$ is sum-free if $x+y\notin S$ for every $x,y\in S$, allowing $x=y$. Prove $\Omega$ has more than $2^n$ distinct sum-free subsets.

## Q8 | Equal chords from altitudes | geometry

In triangle $ABC$, $\angle BAC<90^\circ$. The perpendiculars from $C$ to $AB$ and from $B$ to $AC$ meet the circumcircle again at $D,E$, respectively. If $|DE|=|BC|$, find $\angle BAC$.

## Q9 | Many integer roots | algebra, number-theory

Nonconstant integer-coefficient polynomials $p,q$ satisfy that $p(x)q(x)-2015$ has at least $33$ distinct integer roots. Prove neither $p$ nor $q$ has degree less than three.

## Q10 | Binomial moment inequality | inequalities, combinatorics

Prove for all nonnegative integers $j,n$ that
$$\sum_{k=0}^n k^j\binom nk\ge2^{n-j}n^j.$$
