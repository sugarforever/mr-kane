# IrMO 2016 · 26 April 2016

## Q1 | Cyclic digits divisible by 27 | number-theory

If the three-digit number $\overline{ABC}$ is divisible by $27$, prove the three-digit numbers $\overline{BCA},\overline{CAB}$ are also divisible by $27$.

## Q2 | Equal angle bisector segments | geometry

In triangle $ABC$ with $|AB|\ne|AC|$, the bisectors of $\angle ABC,\angle ACB$ meet $AC,AB$ at $E,F$, respectively, and intersect at $I$. If $|EI|=|FI|$, find $\angle BAC$.

## Q3 | Polynomial root existence | algebra

Do there exist four real-coefficient polynomials $P_1,P_2,P_3,P_4$ such that the sum of any three has a real root but the sum of any two has no real root?

## Q4 | Internal and external bisectors | geometry

In triangle $ABC$, $|AC|\ne|BC|$. The internal and external angle bisectors at $C$ meet line $AB$ at $P,Q$, with $P$ between $A,B$. Prove that for any point $M$ on the circle with diameter $PQ$, $\angle AMP=\angle BMP$.

## Q5 | Integer geometric mean bound | inequalities, number-theory

Positive integers $a_1,\ldots,a_m$, none equal to $10$, satisfy $a_1+\cdots+a_m=10m$. Prove
$$(a_1a_2\cdots a_m)^{1/m}\le3\sqrt{11}.$$

## Q6 | Centroid right angle | geometry

Triangle $ABC$ has sides $a=|BC|>b=|AC|$. Points $K,H$ on segment $BC$ satisfy $|CH|=(a+b)/3$, $|CK|=(a-b)/3$. If $G$ is the centroid, prove $\angle KGH=90^\circ$.

## Q7 | Distinct entries in four rows | combinatorics

A rectangular array of positive integers has four rows. Every column sums to $20$, and all entries within a row are distinct. What is the maximum number of columns?

## Q8 | Linear system identity | algebra

For real $a,b,c$ with $abc\ne0$, determine $x,y,z$ such that
$$bz+cy=a,\quad cx+az=b,\quad ay+bx=c.$$
Prove also
$$\frac{1-x^2}{a^2}=\frac{1-y^2}{b^2}=\frac{1-z^2}{c^2}.$$

## Q9 | Nested cube roots | algebra

Show the following number is an integer and find its value:
$$\left(\frac{251}{\frac{1}{\sqrt[3]{252}-5\sqrt[3]{2}}-10\sqrt[3]{63}}+\frac{1}{\frac{251}{\sqrt[3]{252}+5\sqrt[3]{2}}+10\sqrt[3]{63}}\right)^3.$$

## Q10 | Nine point circle | geometry

Let $AE$ be a diameter of the circumcircle of triangle $ABC$. Join $E$ to its orthocentre $H$ and extend $EH$ to meet the circle again at $D$. Prove the nine point circle of $ABC$ passes through the midpoint of $HD$.

The nine point circle passes through the side midpoints, altitude feet and midpoints of the segments joining the orthocentre to the vertices.
