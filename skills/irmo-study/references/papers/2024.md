# IrMO 2024 · 27 January 2024

## Q1 | Runcible integers | number-theory

The positive integers $1,2$ are runcible. If $a,b$ are runcible, not necessarily distinct, then $2a+3b$ is runcible. Is $2024$ runcible?

## Q2 | Squares between binomial values | number-theory, combinatorics

A nonnegative integer $p$ is a 3-choice if $p=k(k-1)(k-2)/6$ for some positive integer $k$. For 3-choices $p<q$, show there is an integer $n$ with $p\le n^2<q$.

## Q3 | Positive integer functional equation | algebra, number-theory

Let $\mathbb Z_+=\{1,2,\ldots\}$. Find all $f:\mathbb Z_+\to\mathbb Z_+$ such that
$$f(mn)+1=f(m)+f(n)\quad(m,n\in\mathbb Z_+),$$
$$f(2024)=1,\qquad f(n)=1\quad\text{for every positive }n\equiv22\pmod{23}.$$

## Q4 | Equal digit differences | combinatorics

How many four-digit numbers $\overline{ABCD}$ satisfy $|A-B|=|B-C|=|C-D|$? The leading digit $A$ cannot be zero.

## Q5 | Tangency from a chord midpoint | geometry

$A,B,C$ lie on circle $\Gamma$, and $L$ is the midpoint of $BC$. The perpendicular bisector of $BC$ meets $\Gamma$ at $M,N$, with $A,M$ on opposite sides of $BC$. Let $S=BC\cap AM$ using segments. Line $NS$ meets the circumcircle of $ALM$ at $D,E$, with $D$ inside $\Gamma$. Prove:

(a) $M$ is the circumcentre of $BCD$.

(b) The circumcircles of $BCD,ADN$ are tangent at $D$.

## Q6 | Binomial sum a power of two | number-theory, combinatorics

Find all positive integers $n,m$ such that
$$\binom{n}{1}+\binom{n}{3}=2^m.$$

## Q7 | Coin operation invariants | combinatorics, number-theory

Start with one head and one tail on a table. At each turn choose one move:

(a) Turn over every coin.

(b) Triple both the number of heads and the number of tails.

(c) If there are at least four tails, turn over four tails.

(d) If there are at least five tails, turn over three tails and discard two tails.

If the final number of heads is $2024$, what are all possible final numbers of tails?

## Q8 | Product inequality | inequalities

For positive real $a,b,c$ with $a\le c$ and $b\le c$, prove
$$(a+10b)(b+22c)(c+7a)\ge2024abc.$$

## Q9 | Parallelogram cyclicity criterion | geometry

Let $K,L,M$ lie on sides $BC,AB,AC$, respectively, of triangle $ABC$, with $ALKM$ a parallelogram. Choose $S,T$ on lines $KL,KM$, respectively, so $AKBS,AKCT$ are cyclic. Prove $MLST$ is cyclic if and only if $K$ is the midpoint of $BC$.

## Q10 | Nested positive integer function | algebra

Find with proof all $f:\mathbb Z_+\to\mathbb Z_+$ satisfying
$$f(x+f(y)+f(f(z)))=z+f(y)+f(f(x))$$
for all positive integers $x,y,z$.
