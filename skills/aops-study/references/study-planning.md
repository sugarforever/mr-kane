# Planning studies

Use the subject routes in curriculum.md and actual chapter/section content from catalog.md. Establish the goal (school support, mathematical maturity, contest preparation, or a named exam), present knowledge, time budget, and deadline from context. Ask only for missing details that would materially change the plan; offer a provisional first step while waiting. Do not assume a particular contest or invent its current syllabus.

Place the student using a few short tasks that sample prerequisites. Treat placement as provisional. For a Grade 10 student starting algebra and number theory, Volume 1 is the default foundation; a student who already handles it confidently can move into the corresponding Volume 2 route.

A useful plan gives:

- A sequence of named sections with prerequisites and a concrete learning objective for each stage.
- A sustainable weekly allocation for reading, worked examples, independent practice and review. State assumptions about time and adjust for school workload.
- Exercises chosen by reading the actual statements; identify book problems by volume and number, and examples/exercises by chapter label. If no problem was inspected, assign a type of practice rather than inventing a book number.
- A progress checkpoint based on explaining the idea, solving familiar tasks independently and transferring it to a less familiar task. Set advancement or review criteria suitable for the student, not a universal arbitrary score.
- A recovery route for missed weeks or weak prerequisites. Repeated errors should change the plan.

For two concurrent tracks, share the proof foundation and schedule algebra and number theory separately. Keep an error log focused on the cause of the mistake and the corrective action. Revisit missed ideas after a delay and mix older material into later work.

The books do not establish a student's competition ranking, exact study speed, or readiness for a current external examination. Make those distinctions explicit when relevant. Browse official exam sources if the user requests current requirements.
